CI (`.github/workflows/ci.yml`) runs ruff, the full backend suite on SQLite with coverage and 150 fuzzing
seeds (3000 nightly), migrations (`upgrade head`, `alembic check`, downgrade/upgrade round trip) and the
platform tests on PostgreSQL 16, dependency audits with SBOMs for backend and frontend, and the frontend
typecheck and production build. Installs use `backend/constraints.txt` (tested versions).

Regression policy: every defect found (e.g. the OR-Tools abort with a single worker and hint repair,
labour-pool capacity during breaks, detached setups, MRP demand beyond the horizon) is fixed at the
root cause and covered by a test or by the acceptance dataset.
